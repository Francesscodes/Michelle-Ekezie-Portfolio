About me

Celebrating the Art of Sweet Creation

Michelle Ekezie, CEO of Chelle's Dessert, is a visionary entrepreneur passionate about creating unforgettable dessert experiences. With a unique blend of creativity, culinary expertise, and business acumen, Michelle transforms simple ingredients into beautifully crafted cakes, pastries, and desserts that delight both the eyes and the taste buds.

Her work goes beyond baking; it's about storytelling through flavor and presentation. Every creation reflects her commitment to quality, innovation, and the joy that comes from indulging in a perfectly made dessert. By combining her artistic vision with a deep understanding of her audience, Michelle has positioned Chelle's Dessert as a leading name in premium desserts, custom cakes, and celebration treats.

Through Chelle's Dessert, Michelle aims to inspire, connect, and bring people together, making every occasion sweeter and every dessert a memorable experience. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, corporate event, or special celebration, her desserts are designed to leave a lasting impression.

A Symphony of Dessert Delight

Experience the perfect harmony of flavors with Chelle's Dessert's signature Tiramisu. Each bite combines rich mascarpone cream, delicate espresso-soaked ladyfingers, and a dusting of cocoa, creating a dessert that is both luxurious and indulgent. Perfect for special occasions, intimate gatherings, or as a decadent treat, this Tiramisu embodies artistry and premium quality, making it a standout in any dessert spread.

Custom Cakes for Every Occasion

From elegant wedding cakes to fun birthday celebrations, Chelle's Dessert creates stunning custom cakes that are as delicious as they are beautiful. Each cake is crafted with premium ingredients and meticulous attention to detail, ensuring your special moment is perfectly complemented by a show-stopping centerpiece. Whether you prefer classic flavors like red velvet or want to explore unique combinations, we bring your vision to life.

Pastries & Confectioneries

Indulge in our delightful selection of pastries and confectioneries, perfect for any time of day. From flaky croissants and buttery Danish pastries to delicate macarons and artisanal chocolates, each creation showcases expert craftsmanship and premium ingredients. Our small chops and finger foods are ideal for events, parties, and corporate gatherings, offering a perfect blend of taste and presentation.

Full-Service Catering

Make your event unforgettable with Chelle's Dessert catering services. We provide comprehensive catering solutions for weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, and special celebrations. Our team handles everything from menu planning to presentation, ensuring your guests enjoy a memorable culinary experience. We specialize in dessert tables, buffet spreads, and custom meal packages tailored to your needs and preferences.
